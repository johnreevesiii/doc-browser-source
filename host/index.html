Open the game from your stable

The game starts from your stable page. Go to your stable and press Play in browser on one of your browser horses.

Race: the announcer, crowd and music. Seat: your seat's own sounds. Kept on this device; the speaker button mutes both.

Your horse runs on seat 1 of a five-board linked cabinet in this page. Keys (the same as DOC Online unless you change them under Controls): I inserts your card, Z is OK, X is Hold, C is the whip, arrows move, Enter is start. M opens or closes this panel, F is fullscreen. A controller works too.

Controls

Press a button below, then the key or controller button you want. Escape cancels. M, F, Escape and Tab stay the page's own keys. Kept on this device.

Send feedback

Sent with your note: your browser's name and version, the game speed, how many boards loaded, the link state, the last error message on this page and the page version. Nothing else about you or your device.
